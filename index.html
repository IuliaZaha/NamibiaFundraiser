About Our Trip

Hi, I'm Julia!

Thanks to your support last year, we were able to deliver vital resources and financial gifts to YWAM base in Windhoek in our 2 weeks trip in October 2025. It was a milestone, but the journey isn't over.

Real change requires consistency. In February 2027, we are heading back to deepen our relationships within the community and advance the local projects. Will you partner with us again to see this vision through?

Update 2 · Nov 2026New UI
2-minute read, 30 seconds if you skim

Every minute on paperwork is a minute not spent with a child.I'm building the app to give those minutes back.

Kids Club alone welcomes 400+ children every week. The leaders who run it are buried in paper attendance sheets and messy spreadsheets and losing precious time. I can't be at the base every week, but I can build something that lifts the admin off their shoulders. That is why this app exists.

Today

Admin eats the time meant for the community.

  • Paper attendance sheets
  • Long queues of kids at school
  • Slow, repetitive admin after every class

With the app

Leaders spend their energy on people, not forms.

  • One tap to check a child in (either via button or QR code)
  • Works offline, syncs later
  • Reports exported in .csv
